Free lesson resources for nonspeaking, minimally speaking, and unreliably speaking spellers building access to communication through purposeful motor practice.

These lessons are designed for learners whose speech may not reliably show what they know. Each story supports spelling or typing as communication, presumes competence, and gives practitioners and families a structured way to pair rich, novel content with regulation, motor planning, and meaningful response. Use the library as a starting point for letterboards, keyboards, or your preferred communication method.

The goal is not performance for its own sake; it is access to thought, education, relationship, and self-directed participation.
