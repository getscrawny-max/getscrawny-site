Scrawny's Library

Scrawny's Library is my collection of free educational lessons and read-along videos. I began writing lessons during my years as a spelling provider, and I continue sharing them as a thank-you to the individuals and families I've worked with—including athletes I now coach through Get Scrawny.

Each lesson is carefully written around engaging subjects, thoughtful questions, and the presumption of competence. Click the little red video button beneath a lesson for a read-along version by yours truly. :)

The lessons follow a question-and-answer format familiar to families and practitioners who use the Spellers Method/S2C. These materials are offered as free educational resources, separate from Get Scrawny's adaptive personal-training services.

Get Scrawny does not provide spelling instruction, communication therapy, or S2C sessions. Our paid coaching focuses exclusively on adaptive fitness, physical exercise, and intentional movement.
