Adaptive Personal Training

Individualized adaptive personal training for teenagers and adults with motor-planning and movement challenges. Through strength training, coordination exercises, balance activities, and intentional movement, Get Scrawny helps clients build physical skills in a supportive, one-on-one environment.

Personal training adapted to how you move.

For individuals with apraxia or other motor-planning challenges, knowing what they want to do and physically carrying out that movement aren't always the same thing. Our athletes may understand an exercise and want to participate, but need additional time, repetition, or the movement broken down into smaller, accessible steps. That's where Get Scrawny comes in.

Through individualized strength training, balance exercises, coordination activities, and functional movement practice, we help athletes develop greater confidence and consistency in how they move.

Each session is one-on-one and appointment-only at our Oceanside studio. Appointments are scheduled as one-hour blocks with approximately 45–50 minutes of active coaching, leaving room for transitions, regulation, communication, feedback, and individual pacing.

Regulation and readiness always come first.

Get Scrawny provides exercise-based coaching, not occupational therapy, physical therapy, or communication instruction. We welcome collaboration with each athlete's existing support team.

Our work focuses on motor initiation, sequencing, coordination, balance, strength, endurance, and physical participation, with the goal of supporting greater independence in everyday movement.

01

Motor Planning

Tailored to your needs

Clear exercise setup, modeling, rhythm, repetition, and body-based cues help athletes practice initiating, sequencing, and completing movements.

We work toward decreasing support while adapting exercises to each athlete's strengths. The goal is to develop greater control, consistency, and independence in the movement itself.

02

Functional Movement

Our athletes can move!

Through strength training, balance exercises, coordination activities, and functional movement practice, athletes build physical skills that support everyday participation.

Equipment such as a functional trainer, dumbbells, resistance bands, and a BOSU balance trainer gives us different ways to adapt exercises to each athlete's abilities and goals.

Moving is one thing. Moving with intention is the next step.

03

Participation

At your own pace

Every session is built around patience, accessibility, autonomy, and respect for each athlete's individual goals.

We will never force someone to perform a movement. Instead, we provide the time, exercise adaptations, encouragement, and support they need to participate comfortably, practice new skills, and progress at their own pace.

And we'll be their biggest cheerleaders along the way.

Everyone deserves support that grows with them.

Get Scrawny provides individualized adaptive personal training for teenagers and adults, including autistic people, nonspeaking and unreliably speaking people, and individuals with apraxia, dyspraxia, developmental disabilities, or acquired disabilities.

Our exercises and movement activities are adapted to each athlete's individual goals, strengths, and needs.

Our coaching philosophy centers around:

  • Presumption of competence
  • Autonomy and consent
  • Adapting to sensory needs
  • Collaboration with families and support teams

I’m Johnny, the Certified Adaptive Personal Trainer behind Get Scrawny. Most people know me as Scrawny Johnny.

I’ve been moving for as long as I can remember. I grew up on the soccer field before transitioning into competitive swimming and water polo through high school and junior college. When that chapter came to an end, my relationship with movement didn’t. I developed a deeper and more intentional interest in strength, conditioning, fitness, and overall health—both inside and outside of the gym.

During college, I worked at a supplement shop inside a gym, where training, nutrition, and community became part of my everyday environment. That experience helped shape the way I approach health today: not simply as an idea, but as something we practice through the way we live and move.

Academically, I studied Kinesiology at Mt. San Antonio College and Speech-Language Pathology at Cal State San Marcos. Those two areas reflected my growing interests in human movement, individual abilities, and the different ways people interact with the world around them.

Beginning in 2020, I spent nearly five years working as a spelling provider at Spellers Center San Diego, supporting nonspeaking individuals through individualized, one-on-one instruction. That experience deepened my understanding of motor-planning challenges, the importance of patient instruction, and the value of respecting each person's autonomy and individual pace.

Although that experience remains an important part of my professional background, my work today is focused on adaptive personal training. Through Get Scrawny, I provide exercise-based coaching centered on physical movement, strength, coordination, balance, and individual fitness goals—not spelling or communication instruction.

My approach combines my lifelong passion for fitness with an appreciation for the different ways people plan, initiate, and execute movement. Whether we're practicing a new exercise, building strength, coordinating multiple steps, or finding a more accessible way to participate, each session is designed around the athlete's abilities, goals, and progress.

The goal is simple: Help each athlete develop greater confidence, control, and independence in how they move.
